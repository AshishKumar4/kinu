2 · Reverse process: generating by denoising, step by step

The toy dataset is 2-D points on a spiral. Forward pushes them into a Gaussian blob. Reverse starts from pure noise and at each step asks the denoiser where a point came from (x̂₀), then takes a small step toward that answer. The denoiser here is the exact optimal one for this dataset. A real model trains a neural net to approximate it.

Turn on x̂₀ and run Reverse. At high noise every particle predicts roughly the average of the data, near the centre. As the noise drops, predictions commit to specific arms of the spiral, so coarse structure appears first and fine detail last. With 5 steps, DDIM still works but DDPM gets blurry.
