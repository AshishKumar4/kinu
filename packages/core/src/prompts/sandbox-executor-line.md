- **sandbox.*** / `runtime: "sandbox"`: your computer, a full Linux machine with its own CPU, memory, disk and desktop. It handles heavier installs, longer-running processes, large clones and builds, bulk data, and user-visible port-listening apps. It provisions on first use. The moment a job outgrows the workspace, move it here.{{#if sized}} It starts at {{defaultSize}} unless the user chose another size; `sandbox.resize(size)` switches between {{sizes}}, and restarts it when it is running.{{/if}}
